The evidence model

Three numbers. Three meanings.

Mixing them into one “progress %” hides the truth. CodeLabX keeps them apart so a recommendation can be explained.

01 · Mint

Mastery

What scoreable outcomes currently suggest you can do. It moves when you complete work that can be checked.

02 · Mint

Confidence

How much weighted evidence supports that estimate. A lucky quiz is not the same as repeated, varied proof.

03 · Amber when due

Freshness

How recently the skill produced strong evidence. Amber means a short retrieval check is due — not a failure.

How it works

A path you actually take.

These are the steps the product performs today. No waitlists, no hidden modules on this page.

  1. 01

    Choose a roadmap

    Machine Learning or Data Structures & Algorithms. You set duration, daily hours, and level before anything is generated.

  2. 02

    Work the next day

    Each roadmap is ordered days. Mark a day complete when you finish it. Optional AI explanations are labeled as assistance, not a grade.

  3. 03

    Record evidence

    Practice problems, assessments, daily challenges, notes, and videos sit on the same account. Assessments keep answer keys on the server.

  4. 04

    Keep the route honest

    If the product later proposes a path change, you see why. Your current roadmap does not rewrite itself in the background.

Trust, stated plainly

Your data. Your evidence. Your route.

Only claims we can stand behind on this codebase. If a guarantee is missing, it is missing on purpose.

Private by default

Roadmaps, notes, assessments, and chat history are owner-scoped. Other learners do not see them.

You can leave

Export your account data as JSON, or delete the account after an explicit confirmation. Logout is POST-only.

AI is assistance

Lesson help, chat, and code review can be incomplete or wrong. Code review does not execute submitted code.

No invented metrics

Dashboards show recorded activity. Sample numbers on this page are labeled illustrative and are not a user profile.
