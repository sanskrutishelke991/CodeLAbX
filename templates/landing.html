Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about CodeLabX. Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out.

Is CodeLabX really free forever?
Yes, 100% free. No credit card required, no hidden charges, no premium tiers. CodeLabX was built by students, for students. We believe quality education should be accessible to everyone. You get access to all features including AI Study Buddy, personalized roadmaps, code review, tests, and everything else — forever.
What programming languages and topics are supported?
We currently support a wide range of topics including:
  • Programming: Python, JavaScript, Java, C++, and more
  • Data Science & ML: Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Neural Networks
  • Computer Science: Data Structures, Algorithms (DSA)
  • Web Development: Django, React, HTML/CSS
  • Databases: SQL, PostgreSQL
And we're constantly adding more topics based on user feedback.
Do I need any prior coding experience?
Absolutely not! CodeLabX is designed for learners at all levels. When you create a roadmap, you can choose from three difficulty levels:
  • Beginner: Perfect if you're just starting out
  • Intermediate: For those with some coding background
  • Advanced: Deep dive into complex topics
The AI adapts to your pace and skill level, so you always learn at the right speed for you.
How does the AI code review work?
Our AI Code Examiner uses Google Gemini to analyze your code in real-time. You paste your code, and within seconds you get:
  • Line-by-line explanation of what your code does
  • Bug detection and error identification
  • Suggestions for optimization and best practices
  • Time and space complexity analysis
  • Alternative approaches to solve the problem
It's like having a senior developer reviewing your code 24/7.
Is my code and data private and secure?
Yes, your privacy is our top priority. Here's what we do:
  • All data is stored securely with industry-standard encryption
  • Your code and progress are visible only to you
  • We never sell or share your data with third parties
  • No tracking, no ads, no marketing spam
  • You can delete your account and all data anytime
We believe learning should be a safe and private experience.
Can I use CodeLabX on mobile devices?
Absolutely! CodeLabX is fully responsive and works beautifully on:
  • Desktop computers
  • Laptops
  • Tablets
  • Mobile phones
You can start learning on your laptop and continue on your phone. Your progress syncs automatically across all devices. A dedicated mobile app is coming soon!
